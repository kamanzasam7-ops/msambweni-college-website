About Msambweni TVC

Msambweni Technical and Vocational College is a centre of excellence offering hands‑on training in ICT, Electrical, Plumbing, and other technical fields. We prepare students for industry with modern workshops and experienced lecturers.

📚 Comprehensive Department Details

Electrical

Our electrical engineering program offers intensive training in domestic and industrial wiring, motor controls, renewable energy systems, and electrical machine maintenance. Students work on live projects and gain experience with modern test equipment. Graduates are prepared for licensing and immediate employment.

→ Diploma & Certificate options

Hospitality

Comprehensive training in front office operations, housekeeping, food production, and event planning. The department features a mock hotel and training restaurant. Students undertake internships in leading hotels and graduate with strong customer service skills.
