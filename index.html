📚 Comprehensive Department Details

Electrical

Our electrical engineering program offers intensive training in domestic and industrial wiring, motor controls, renewable energy systems, and electrical machine maintenance. Students work on live projects and gain experience with modern test equipment. Graduates are prepared for licensing and immediate employment.

→ Diploma & Certificate options

Hospitality

Comprehensive training in front office operations, housekeeping, food production, and event planning. The department features a mock hotel and training restaurant. Students undertake internships in leading hotels and graduate with strong customer service skills.

→ Industry partnerships

Mechanical

State-of-the-art workshop for fitting, machining, welding, and fabrication. Students learn to operate lathes, milling machines, and CNC equipment. Strong focus on industrial attachments and real-world projects.

→ Diploma in Mechanical Engineering (Production)

Plumbing

Comprehensive plumbing technology covering water supply systems, drainage, sanitation, and pipe fitting. Practical sessions on rigs and community projects. Graduates ready for construction and maintenance sectors.

→ Artisan, Certificate & Diploma

ICT

Modern computing labs with networking, programming (Python, Java), web design, and hardware repair. Emphasis on industry certifications (Cisco, CompTIA) and innovation.

→ Diploma in ICT, Certificate in Computer Packages

Masonry

Hands-on bricklaying, blockwork, concreting, plastering, and tiling. Students construct actual campus structures under supervision. Strong linkage with construction companies.

→ Certificate in Masonry, Artisan

Library & Info Science

Training in library management, classification, digital archiving, and information systems. Prepares students for work in academic, public, and digital libraries.

→ Diploma in Library and Information Science
