About Msambweni TVC

Msambweni Technical and Vocational College is a centre of excellence offering hands‑on training in ICT, Electrical, Plumbing, and other technical fields. We prepare students for industry with modern workshops and experienced lecturers.

Our Departments & Activities

ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING

Learn installation, maintenance, and repair of electrical systems. Our workshop is equipped with modern tools for hands‑on training in industrial and domestic wiring.

HOSPITALITY MANAGEMENT

Front office, housekeeping, food production, and event management. Students train in a simulated hotel environment to meet industry standards.

MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

Fitting, machining, welding, and fabrication. Our workshop features lathes, milling machines, and CNC equipment for real-world skills.

PLUMBING
