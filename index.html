📚 Comprehensive Department Details

Electrical

Our electrical engineering program offers intensive training in domestic and industrial wiring, motor controls, renewable energy systems, and electrical machine maintenance. Students work on live projects and gain experience with modern test equipment. Graduates are prepared for licensing and immediate employment.

→ Diploma & Certificate options

Hospitality

Comprehensive training in front office operations, housekeeping, food production, and event planning. The department features a mock hotel and training restaurant. Students undertake internships in leading hotels and graduate with strong customer service skills.

→ Industry partnerships

Mechanical

Fitting, machining, welding, and CNC operation are core areas. The workshop is equipped with lathes, milling machines, and modern fabrication tools. Students learn to read engineering drawings and produce precision components.

→ Hands-on projects

Plumbing

From basic pipe fitting to advanced sanitary systems and water supply design. Training includes installation of solar water heaters, pump systems, and modern plumbing codes. Graduates are in high demand in construction and maintenance sectors.

→ Certification by KNEC

Masonry

Comprehensive bricklaying, blockwork, plastering, and concrete construction. Students learn to interpret building plans and execute both traditional and modern finishing techniques. Safety and site management are emphasized.

→ Practical site exposure

ICT

Programming (Python, Java), networking (Cisco), cybersecurity, and web development. Our labs run the latest software and hardware. Students can earn industry certifications alongside their diploma.

→ CCNA & ethical hacking modules

Food & Beverage

Culinary arts, bakery, and beverage management. The training kitchen is equipped to industrial standards. Students learn menu planning, cost control, and international cuisine. Opportunities for internships in top restaurants.

→ Professional chef training

Cosmetology

Hair styling, skincare, makeup, and salon management. Students practice in a full-service salon environment. The curriculum includes product knowledge, sanitation, and client consultation. Preparation for state board exams.

→ Latest beauty techniques

Hairdressing

Specialized training in cutting, coloring, perming, and styling. Emphasis on creativity and trend awareness. Students work with mannequins and live models, building a portfolio for employment.

→ Salon management skills
