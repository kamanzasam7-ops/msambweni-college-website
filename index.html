About Msambweni TVC

Msambweni Technical and Vocational College is a centre of excellence offering hands‑on training in ICT, Electrical, Plumbing, and other technical fields. We prepare students for industry with modern workshops and experienced lecturers.

Our Departments & Activities

COMMUNICATION & INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY

ICT (information and communications technology) is the infrastructure and components that enable modern computing. Among the goals of IC technologies, tools and systems is to improve the way humans create, process and share data or information with each other.

Download Brochure (PDF)

ELECTRICAL DEPARTMENT

Students enrolled in the Electrical Engineering program gain skills in electrical installation, troubleshooting, maintenance, and instrumentation. They learn to diagnose faults, maintain equipment, and master measurement & control of electrical quantities in residential, commercial, and industrial settings.

Download Brochure (PDF)

PLUMBING

The Diploma in Plumbing equips students with advanced knowledge in water supply, drainage systems, pipe fitting, sanitary installations, and safety standards. It includes modern technologies like renewable energy systems and building codes, preparing professionals for real-world challenges.

Download Brochure (PDF)

TREE PLANTING DAY

This is a simple, safe, and effective way of protecting people against harmful diseases, before they come into contact — and a step toward a greener, healthier campus community. Join our environmental conservation efforts.
